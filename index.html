Republic of Texas

Ten Years a Nation

It’s 1836. Texas just won its independence, and now it has to survive as its own country. You’re the president. The treasury is empty, the army is restless, and the whole world is watching what you do next.

Optional. Uses this device’s camera so you can point, pinch, raise your hand, and sign in the air. The video never leaves this device. Mouse and touch always work too.
For teachers

Covers TEKS 7.4(A) and 7.4(B): the Houston, Lamar, and Jones administrations, the Texas Rangers, Chief Bowles, William Goyens, Mary Maverick, José Antonio Navarro, the Córdova Rebellion, the Council House Fight, the Santa Fe Expedition, public debt, and the road to annexation. A full run takes 25 to 35 minutes. Students finish with a one-page record you can have them screenshot or download. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Hand controls need the page opened directly (a Vercel link works). Inside a Google Sites embed the camera is usually blocked, and the page falls back to mouse and touch.
